**CRITICAL DEFINITION — What is a "shot":**
A shot is a segment filmed in ONE CONTINUOUS TAKE without the camera being stopped and restarted.
A shot boundary = a HARD CUT where filming physically stopped and resumed from a different angle/location.

**These are NOT shot boundaries (do NOT split on these):**
- Camera zoom in/out within the same scene
- Camera pan, tilt, or tracking movement
- Subject moving closer or farther from camera
- Change in framing (wide → close-up) via continuous camera movement
- Gradual lighting changes

**These ARE shot boundaries:**
- Instantaneous jump to a completely different scene or location
- Instantaneous jump to a different time (time cut)
- Sudden appearance of entirely different subjects with no transition
